# Gerald on Governance

Operational AI Governance Research, Frameworks, Controls, and Implementation Artifacts.

## Mission

Most AI governance programs fail because they stop at principles and policies.

Gerald on Governance focuses on operationalizing AI governance through frameworks, controls, ownership models, runtime governance mechanisms, auditability, and accountability structures.

## Current Frameworks

- GOAG Framework
- AI Agent Ownership Framework
- AI Runtime Governance Framework
- AI Financial Accountability Stack

## Research Areas

- AI Governance
- AI Risk Management
- AI Auditing
- Runtime Governance
- AI Accountability
- AI Controls
- Enterprise Trust
